Medical laboratory and imaging for Ghana

A Ghanaian laboratory built to a global standard

RayBen Diagnostics is a wholly Ghanaian owned laboratory with working experience in both Ghana and the Netherlands. It was established in 2013 as an international medical diagnostic centre between the two governments, set up to raise the quality of laboratory medicine across Ghana and West Africa.

Ghana sits in a tropical belt where malaria, hepatitis B, tuberculosis and jaundice still take lives that early detection would have saved. We run the majority of those tests in-house so accurate diagnostics stay within reach of an ordinary family.

From booking to report

How a test works here

Four steps, no queueing for hours, and a named person you can call at every stage.

Start with step one

  1. Book

    Message us on WhatsApp, call, or use the form. Tell us the test and where you want the sample taken.

  2. Give the sample

    Walk into any of our four branches, or stay where you are and a collection officer comes to you.

  3. We analyse

    Your sample runs alongside internal controls on accredited platforms and is verified by a scientist before release.

  4. Get your result

    Collected in person, emailed, or sent on WhatsApp — most routine tests the same day. Your doctor can be copied in.

Compliance

Accredited where it counts

RayBen Labs is accredited by AMC ISO 15189 and Labor Dr. Wisplinghoff ISO 15189. That accreditation covers competence of staff, calibration of equipment, handling of samples and traceability of every result — not just the building.

It is the reason a RayBen report is accepted by clinicians, insurers and courts across Ghana and beyond.
